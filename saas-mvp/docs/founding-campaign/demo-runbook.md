# 15-Minute Line Up Demo

## Before The Call

- Open the anonymous demonstration workspace.
- Confirm Home, Library, flashcards, assignment, and readiness pages load.
- Put the prospect’s restaurant name and known operating details in the CRM.
- Know who makes the decision and who would provide source material.

## Minute 0–3: Diagnose

Ask where training lives, how staff learn new items, how readiness is verified, and what takes managers the most time. Reflect the problem back in one sentence before showing the product.

## Minute 3–8: Show The Staff Experience

1. Open Home and explain new or assigned training.
2. Complete one flashcard interaction.
3. Open Library and find one training page by section.
4. Show the mobile layout.

Say: “Staff only need to know two places: Home to practice and Library to look something up.”

## Minute 8–11: Show Management Visibility

1. Show restaurant branding and sections.
2. Show Team & Assignments.
3. Show Readiness and explain current versus needs review.

Say: “You can see who is studying, but you do not have to build the library. That is the work we take off your plate.”

## Minute 11–13: Explain Delivery

1. Restaurant sends logo, menus, tech sheets, beverage specs, SOPs, and employee roles.
2. Line Up organizes the workspace and creates study facts.
3. One restaurant leader reviews the result.
4. Line Up invites two test users, then launches the staff team.
5. Routine updates continue through the monthly service.

## Minute 13–15: Close

Ask who else needs to approve, whether source material can be shared this week, and whether anything prevents a $500 kickoff.

Close with: “The next step is the $500 setup payment and material checklist. We deliver within seven business days after complete material arrives. Your $99 monthly service begins 30 days after launch and remains month-to-month.”

Record one outcome in the CRM: qualified, nurture, not a fit, or won.
