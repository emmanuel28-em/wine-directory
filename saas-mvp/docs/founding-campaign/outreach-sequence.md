# Founding Restaurant Outreach Sequence

Personalize every message with one real detail: a menu change, beverage program, multiple dayparts, recent opening, hiring signal, or known operating complexity.

## First Email — Day 1

**Subject:** I’ll organize [Restaurant]’s training material for you

Hi [First Name],

I’m a NYC restaurant floor manager and the founder of Line Up. I built it after seeing how difficult it is to keep menu notes, wine sheets, SOPs, and staff updates organized.

You send me the material your team already uses. I build a branded training portal where staff can study it, practice with flashcards, and managers can see readiness. You do not have to build courses or learn complicated software.

I’m offering this to three NYC restaurants for a $500 setup fee and $99/month after the first 30 days. Would a 15-minute walkthrough be useful?

Emmanuel Morales  
Founder, Line Up  
https://lineuptraining.com/founding-restaurants

## Follow-Up — Day 4

**Subject:** Re: [Restaurant]’s training material

Hi [First Name],

I know restaurant managers do not need another system to configure. The difference with Line Up is that I handle the setup and ongoing updates. If you send the existing documents, I return an organized staff training portal.

Would a quick look next week be useful?

Emmanuel

## Final Follow-Up — Day 8

**Subject:** Close the loop?

Hi [First Name],

Should I close the loop, or would seeing a working anonymous restaurant example be helpful?

Emmanuel
