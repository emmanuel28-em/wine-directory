# Founding Restaurant Onboarding Checklist

## Sale And Kickoff

- [ ] Decision-maker and operational champion identified.
- [ ] Service agreement signed.
- [ ] $500 setup payment received.
- [ ] Primary contact and billing contact confirmed.
- [ ] Launch target recorded in the CRM.

## Material Intake

- [ ] Restaurant legal name and public-facing name.
- [ ] Logo with permission to display it.
- [ ] Address, website, and primary contact information.
- [ ] Current menus and menu section names.
- [ ] Food tech sheets and allergy information.
- [ ] Wine, cocktail, beer, spirits, or beverage materials.
- [ ] SOPs, service standards, and onboarding documents.
- [ ] Staff roles or groups.
- [ ] Archive list for outdated information.
- [ ] One person authorized to approve content.

## Workspace Build

- [ ] Create the restaurant tenant and account owner.
- [ ] Confirm restaurant name and logo appear correctly.
- [ ] Create collections matching the restaurant’s vocabulary.
- [ ] Import and clean training pages.
- [ ] Separate published, draft, and archived information.
- [ ] Generate five useful study facts per current page.
- [ ] Review allergens, ingredients, one-liners, and service notes.
- [ ] Confirm all images load without cropping.
- [ ] Confirm no content from another restaurant is visible.

## Pilot Review

- [ ] Invite the account owner and two test users.
- [ ] Test Home, Library, flashcards, search, assignments, and readiness on mobile.
- [ ] Obtain written content approval.
- [ ] Correct issues before full staff launch.
- [ ] Run the 20-minute manager handoff.

## Launch And Follow-Up

- [ ] Invite the staff team.
- [ ] Assign the first required training update.
- [ ] Confirm at least one staff member completes five facts.
- [ ] Confirm readiness appears for management.
- [ ] Schedule the seven-day adoption check.
- [ ] Schedule the 30-day review.
- [ ] Confirm $99 monthly billing begins after the adoption period.
- [ ] Ask for feedback and permission to create a named case study.
