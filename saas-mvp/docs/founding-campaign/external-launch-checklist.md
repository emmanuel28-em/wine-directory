# External Launch Checklist

The campaign page and demo-request form work without these services. Complete the items below before broad outreach so a qualified lead can move from reply to kickoff without delay.

## 1. Demo Scheduling

Create one 15-minute Google Calendar appointment schedule.

- Name: `Line Up Restaurant Training Demo`
- Duration: 15 minutes
- Location: Google Meet
- Availability: two or three repeatable blocks outside service hours
- Description: `A quick look at how Line Up turns existing restaurant materials into an organized staff training portal.`
- Questions: restaurant name, role, employee count, and where training material lives today

The website already collects a preferred contact time and securely stores the request. A booking URL can later be offered as a second scheduling option without removing this form.

## 2. Founding Setup Payment

In Stripe, create a one-time product named `Line Up Founding Restaurant Setup` priced at `$500`.

- Create a Stripe Payment Link for the product.
- Collect customer name, email, restaurant name, and billing address.
- Keep the link private until a restaurant completes the demo and agrees to the scope.
- Record payment in the campaign CRM under `Setup Paid?`.

The Line Up Starter subscription remains `$99/month`. Start it after the restaurant's 30-day adoption period, not on the setup-payment date.

## 3. Agreement Review

Customize `founding-customer-agreement.md` and have a qualified attorney review it before repeated use. Confirm the scope, one-location limit, update allowance, cancellation terms, data ownership, and permission to use a restaurant name or screenshots.

## 4. Proof And Walkthrough

- Record the 90-second walkthrough using an anonymous demo workspace.
- Do not display Rezdora's name, logo, documents, staff, or private usage data without written permission.
- Follow `walkthrough-script.md` and keep the final recording under two minutes.

## 5. Before The First Email

- Open `/founding-restaurants` on desktop and mobile.
- Submit one clearly labeled internal test request.
- Confirm it appears in Platform Control under managed-setup inquiries.
- Open the PDF offer from the campaign page.
- Add the first 10 qualified restaurants to the CRM.
- Test the Stripe setup link and demo-booking page in an incognito window.
