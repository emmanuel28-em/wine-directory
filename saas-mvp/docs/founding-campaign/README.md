# Line Up Founding Restaurant Campaign

This folder is the operating kit for signing the first three NYC restaurant customers.

Complete `external-launch-checklist.md` before broad outreach. It covers the Calendar, Stripe, legal, and proof steps that require owner decisions outside the application.

## Offer

- $500 one-time setup fee, paid at kickoff.
- One restaurant location.
- Branded restaurant workspace powered by Line Up.
- Delivery within seven business days after complete source materials arrive.
- 30-day adoption period after launch.
- $99 monthly maintenance begins after the adoption period.
- Month-to-month service.
- Routine updates are included; major restructures, extra locations, and unusual migrations are quoted separately.

## Campaign Goal

Run the campaign for six weeks using approximately 10 founder hours each week.

| Funnel stage | Six-week target |
| --- | ---: |
| Decision-makers contacted | 120 |
| Qualified replies | 12 |
| Demos held | 8 |
| Proposals sent | 5 |
| Founding restaurants won | 3 |
| Setup revenue | $1,500 |
| Monthly recurring revenue | $297 |

## Weekly Operating Rhythm

| Day | Activity |
| --- | --- |
| Monday | Research 10 restaurants and identify two decision-makers at each. |
| Tuesday | Send 20 personalized first-touch emails. |
| Wednesday | Connect or follow up through LinkedIn or Instagram. |
| Thursday | Hold demos and make selective calls or visits outside service hours. |
| Friday | Send follow-ups, update the CRM, and prepare proposals. |

## Qualification

Contact a broad mix of NYC restaurants, but prioritize accounts showing at least three of these signals:

- 20 or more employees.
- Frequent menu or special changes.
- Serious wine or cocktail program.
- Multiple menus or dayparts.
- Active hiring or location growth.
- Training split between Docs, PDFs, messages, binders, and verbal pre-shifts.
- Existing use of Sling, 7shifts, Google Drive, Slack, or similar tools.

Decision-maker order:

1. General Manager or Director of Operations.
2. AGM, Service Director, Beverage Director, or Training Manager.
3. Founder or owner of an independent restaurant.
4. Chef-owner when they directly control training and operations.

## Adjustment Rules

- Fewer than three positive replies after 40 contacts: revise the subject line and account qualification.
- Demos without proposals: strengthen the anonymous proof and clarify the done-for-you outcome.
- Proposals without closes: ask directly whether trust, price, timing, or implementation is blocking the decision.
- Onboarding takes more than one working day: tighten the fair-use scope before selling customer four.
