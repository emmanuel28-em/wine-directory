## 1. Service

Line Up will configure one restaurant-branded training workspace for one location using materials supplied by the Customer. The workspace may include training sections, training pages, staff study facts, invitations, assignments, and readiness reporting.

## 2. Setup

The Customer will pay a non-refundable $500 setup fee at kickoff. Line Up will target delivery within seven business days after receiving complete source materials, branding, and requested access information. A delivery date may move when materials are incomplete, illegible, or changed during setup.

## 3. Monthly Service

The Customer receives a 30-day adoption period beginning on the workspace launch date. After that period, service costs $99 per month and renews monthly until canceled. Routine content updates are included. Major library restructures, additional locations, custom integrations, or unusually large migrations require a separate written quote.

## 4. Customer Responsibilities

The Customer confirms it has the right to provide all submitted materials and is responsible for reviewing content accuracy before staff rely on it. The Customer will name an authorized contact for approvals, corrections, employee access, and billing.

## 5. Content And Branding

The Customer retains ownership of its menus, recipes, specifications, procedures, logos, and other supplied content. Line Up retains ownership of the software, platform design, code, workflows, and reusable system components. The workspace is restaurant-branded and powered by Line Up; it is not a transfer of the underlying software.

## 6. Confidentiality And Access

Each party will use reasonable care with non-public business information. The Customer controls which employees are invited. Line Up may access the workspace when necessary to perform setup, maintenance, or support.

## 7. Cancellation

Either party may cancel the monthly service before the next renewal date. Paid setup work and completed subscription periods are not refundable. On request, Line Up will provide a reasonable export of Customer-authored training text before account closure.

## 8. Service Limits

Line Up is a training and information-management tool. It does not replace required food-safety certification, legal advice, medical advice, allergy controls, management supervision, or the Customer’s responsibility to verify operational information.
